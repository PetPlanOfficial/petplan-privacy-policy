Privacy Policy for PetPlan

Last updated: March 5, 2026

PetPlan ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

PetPlan may collect account information such as email address and username when users create an account.

The app may also store pet information including pet names, pet photos, reminders, schedules, and expense records.

How We Use Information

This information is used to provide pet care tracking features, manage accounts, and send reminder notifications.

Third Party Services

PetPlan uses Firebase to securely store and manage data. Authentication may also be provided through Apple Sign In or Google Sign In.

Account Deletion

Users can delete their account directly within the app. When an account is deleted, associated personal data will also be removed.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: petplansupport@gmail.com
