Privacy Policy for PetPlan

Last updated: March 5, 2026

PetPlan ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how PetPlan collects, uses, and protects information when you use the PetPlan mobile application (the "App").

By using PetPlan, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When using PetPlan, we may collect the following types of information:

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Authentication information when signing in using Apple or Google

This information is used to create and manage your account.

User Content

The App allows users to store information related to their pets. This may include:

  • Pet name
  • Pet details (such as breed or age)
  • Pet photos
  • Care reminders and schedules
  • Pet-related expense information

This information is stored so that the app can provide its features.

Device Information

We may automatically collect limited device information necessary for the app to function properly, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Provide pet care tracking features
  • Store reminders and schedules
  • Allow users to upload and manage pet photos
  • Send push notifications related to reminders
  • Maintain and improve app performance
  • Provide user support

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

PetPlan uses trusted third-party services to operate the app.

Firebase

We use Firebase to store and manage user data securely. Firebase may process certain information on our behalf in order to provide database, authentication, and cloud services.

Firebase is operated by Google. You can review their privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple Sign In and Google Sign In

If you choose to sign in using Apple or Google, authentication is handled by those services. PetPlan receives only the information necessary to create your account.

4. Push Notifications

PetPlan may send push notifications to remind users about scheduled pet care tasks. Users can disable notifications at any time through their device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data using secure technologies and trusted cloud infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide the services of the app. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our systems.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their account directly within the app. When an account is deleted, associated personal data stored by PetPlan will also be deleted.

8. Children's Privacy

PetPlan is available for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
